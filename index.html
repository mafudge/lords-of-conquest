Credits & provenance

The applet shown here was written by Randy Gettman (circa 2000–2003) as a faithful imitation of Electronic Arts' 1986 game Lords of Conquest. The original applet was hosted at members.cox.net/rgettman and later mirrored at lordsofconquest.angelfire.com. The loc.jar binary served here is the unmodified file recovered from the Internet Archive's capture of the angelfire mirror.

Lords of Conquest is a 1986 game by Electronic Arts, designed by Eon Software (Bill Eberle, Jack Kittredge, Peter Olotka). See the Wikipedia article for the original game's design and history.
